## 0.2.1

- Waist cuts came far too often (playtest: F9 took the waist 137 times to 89-122 for each other part, and both real deaths were waist cuts): the death odds were 30 / 22 / 38 / 10 (limb, head, torso, split), and the blades that go through her body without splitting her, and the `bodyBias` ones, roll them too. Now 40 / 28 / 20 / 12, in `settings.lua` and the README (an installed `settings.lua` keeps its own odds: change them there).
- A killing blade through her body that didn't split her (four times in five) dropped the blade: the death rolled its odds, mostly a waist cut square to her spine, and the arms and legs the weapon visibly went through stayed on (playtest). Now her body is cut along the blade and every limb it crossed near the hit comes off after it (`multiCut`); so does a `bodyBias` waist cut, after the limb the blade met (`her body at ...`, `then ...` in the log). Untested in game.
- Her bleeding sprayed bright red, not the dark crimson of her pools: the game's blood materials take their colour from the global `MPC_BloodParameters`, which `gore.bloodTint` now darkens before each bleed or hit blood is spawned (the game's blood on her too). Untested in game.
- The game stuttered all the time (playtest): the death screen probe below looked for its widget with a scan of every object, 17 ms twice a second, death or not, and `attackDown` scanned for the enemies' knockdown checks every 3 s, 40-50 ms each. The widget is now found once in a death and kept, and the knockdown checks are scanned once, then each new one is caught as it loads.
- After a death she was got up at `recoverAfter` (9 s), under the death screen, whole again behind it. Now she stays as she died until a button on the death screen is pressed (the game respawns her only after that) or the screen goes away (`tune.recoverOn` "button", "timer" for the old way; a death with no death screen gets her up at `recoverAfter` + `screenWait`, 4 s). Untested in game.
- The death screen comes up all black, nothing of the game behind it (playtest). Not found yet: the log now says what covers the view while it is up (`death screen (N s into the death): ...`: the widget's `black` and `FadeOut` images, its blur, the camera's fade).
- A thigh cut on CH_P_EVE_14 left parts of the outfit behind (playtest screenshot): its leg straps were cut off as pieces of their own, as an arm's cape is, and flew 2 m off with their skin torn off the leg, and its laces running down the leg went whole with her body, so the knee's skin stayed glued to her spine and stretched from the severed leg to her. A chain lying along the limb (none of its bodies past the limb's end), never a twist bone (on the next playtest `Ab-L-Thigh-Tw0`, with a body of its own and turned 3 cm off the thigh, was taken for one and kept the thigh's skin on her), is now a strap: over the plane it stays on her body, hung from the body above, under it it goes with the limb. sbslice.dll cuts a hanging piece like the body when its few vertices past the plane reach over 12 cm down the other side on that side's bones (a cape kept on the body still goes whole). Harness: `CAPE=<roots>` names the kept chains; new jobs cut CH_P_EVE_14's thigh at 0.4 and 0.2 with its straps kept (67 visible stretched triangles and 82 knee vertices left on her before, none now). Untested in game.
- Enemies keep attacking her while she is knocked down (`enemies.attackDown`, on; in `settings.lua` and the README). The game's enemy behaviour trees held every attack while she had a knockdown, tumble or get-up effect; those checks are switched off in game, and back on with the setting off (`enemies: they attack her while she is down: N knockdown checks in M of the enemies' decision nodes` in the log). Untested in game.
- A swing that cut an arm or leg first and ran on into her body took the limb alone, unless it was an arm and the blade crossed her spine exactly. Now a leg's goes on into her body too, and so does a blade that passes within `multiCut.bodyGraze` (10) cm beside her spine, through her flank: her body is cut on the spine at the blade's angle (square to it when the blade runs down her length). Untested in game.
- Her arms went through her body in the ragdoll, the upper half's too after a waist split: none of her bodies collided with each other (an arm landing on her once set her spinning). Now her arms collide with her hips and chest and with each other, every other pair still not: sbslice.dll's collision table leaves those pairs out (`physfix.armCollision`, `armGap`: a pair whose bodies already touch as she goes limp is left out, so nothing starts overlapping). - A cut-off arm, leg or head fell through her body: her bodies don't collide with each other. Now each cut adds the piece against the rest of her to the colliding pairs (`physfix.pieceCollision`; `cut piece <bone>: N pairs collide ...` in the log), but the pairs touching where she is cut. sbslice.dll's collision table now takes 256 colliding pairs (was 64). First release.

- Hit marks are parked: `tune.gore.hitMarks.enabled` is false, and the switch is commented out of `settings.default.lua` and the README (with F10). Hits she survives leave nothing, and after-death or corpse hits that don't cut only bleed.
- Versioning: 0.2.0, logged first thing at start (`Stellar Blade Gore Mod 0.2.0`).
- `Scripts/settings.lua`: made from the shipped `settings.default.lua` on the first start, so extracting an update over it never overwrites it. The player's switches (gore, death odds, cuts after death, her voice, enemy gore, dev), laid over `tune.lua` key by key and re-read with F6. `ue4ss/install.sh` keeps an existing one.
