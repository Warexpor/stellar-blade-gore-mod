# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Glass cannon still never found her HP: no hit was seen, and her death check came back `err none at 0, kept 7557` (her HP wasn't 0; her HUD's HP bar read 0.044). The HP bar, `ProgressBar_HP.Percent`, is the one HUD value the game keeps live, so the search now anchors on it: once the bar holds still at a reading under 0.98, `vd<actor>,3` keeps the stats arrays whose HP / MaxHP read it (within 0.015, heals included), and finds her array when one is left; her death checks the bar before HP 0. The widget is kept between reads (FindAllOf every 5 s at most). `test_stats` covers a full bar refused, a bar reading found, a heal in between and two readings telling arrays apart. Untested in game.
- Session 26c: tall dark sheets rose from the pieces in ragdoll deaths with a split (playtest log, CH_P_EVE_10). The copy's head breaks free and spins (up to 8000 deg/s), and three things still followed it: the split lid and the flattened sheet, which rode every spine bone including the head, and the copy's neck skin, which kept part of its vanilla weight on the head (557 vertices). Each half now rides only the spine bones it keeps (`SPLIT_RIDE.of`: the copy's head and the far end of a broken spine are left out of the DLL's sheet bands, the lid, the opening's cap and the spine pieces), the copy's neck skin moves its head weight onto the neck, and bones the bone watch saw moving while she played (animation-driven correctives such as `Ab-AX3`) can't be sinks. Harness: half B flings its head too and nothing it keeps may follow; the posed lid check bends the lid as `skin_lid` does (`LIDRIGID=1` the old rigid pieces), which cleared the pre-existing posed CH_P_EVE_10 failure; `DRIVEN=1` splits fail if a turned corrective is a sink; `test/run.sh` adds CH_P_EVE_10 splits (plain, posed, driven) and a driven CH_P_EVE_09 split. Untested in game.
- A killing swing through her blocking arm took the arm alone: when the arm it took first is in front of her and its plane goes on through her spine below the neck within `multiCut.bodyReach` (60) cm of the hit, her body is cut too, across the chest or the waist along the blade (`multiCut.body`; queued after the arm, before any other limb). Harness: `WAIST=1 ARMFIRST=1` cuts the left upper arm first. Glass cannon follows its rule now: with the shield up hits take the shield only; with it down any hit kills her (an arm no longer just comes off, a leg no longer only takes damage), cut along the enemy weapon's swing when one landed, else where it landed.
- Twenty-fifth playtest. Every cut stuttered and the piece came off a moment after her death: no sbslice command was over 15 ms, but the cut's assets (the bone stub, the cap material, the blood and bleed effects, the sound cues) were held by nothing, so garbage collection freed them between deaths and every cut loaded them again from the pak on the game thread (the cut stage 112 ms, the opening 61, in the new timings). The DLL now keeps every asset gore.lua loads loaded for good (`o<object>`: its RootSet flag in GUObjectArray, written only when the item points back at the object), and they are loaded one every 5 ticks while she plays, so even the first cut loads nothing. A thigh cut's stub flashed all over the screen: its carrier was the spine's `Ab-AX3` again (the thigh's parent is the spine on her rig, so the parent-only rule allowed it), a corrective the animation drives that only held still at the cut. Carriers must now have held still while she played (`w<comp>` every 5 ticks while she is alive; at least 30 looks); the arm test's `DRIVEN=1` swings the parent's helpers once and `UNWATCHED=1` never looks, and neither may carry. Glass cannon never found her HP: no hit on her was seen in the fight she died in (the mesh's hit shake is rare); her death (HP 0) now settles the search, so the mode is on from her second life, with a note when found, and a quiet check drops only what changed on 6 checks running (a combo's hits on two running lost her array). Diagnostics: each cut stage logs when it ran after the cut was asked for and how long it took, step by step past `slowMs`; a tick of the mod's over `slowMs` logs as `slow tick` with its parts; a frame over 70 ms logs as `frame hitch` with the mod's last tick and stage.

Harness: the body opening must cover 12 of 16 directions round the arm after the collapse; the arm test runs on outfits without a cape (CH_P_EVE_30 had been skipped); `ARMBONE`/`ARMEND`/`ARMAT`/`DBGARM`; the flung-arm check compares visible stretched triangles with the still pose's.
