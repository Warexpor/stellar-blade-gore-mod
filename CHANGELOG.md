# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- A death could crash the game inside UE4SS (playtest, 00:25; the same crash 2026-10-21): the mod kept every character it had seen, and the enemies its swing tracking followed, and checked them with `IsValid` each tick. Once the game freed a killed enemy, that check read freed memory, which no Lua error handling catches. Engine tweak packs that hold freed objects for 10 minutes (`gc.TimeBetweenPurgingPendingKillObjects=600`) hid it. Held characters, followed enemies and their weapons, the enemies' knockdown checks and the effects and decals on cut enemies are now kept by their path and checked in the game's name table, so nothing the game freed is read again. Untested in game.
- With Ultra Violence, its blood stayed on her after a death's respawn (reported): it clears her only at a load, a camp rest or in water, and the game's respawn is none of these. As the mod gets her up for a respawn, her movement reads as swimming for 1.2 s (its own check runs every 0.5 s), so it clears her (`swim clear` in UE4SS.log, `Ultra Violence's blood: ...` in ours); then she walks again. `uvClean = false` in `tune.lua` leaves its blood on. A revive in place keeps it. Untested in game.
- With `dev` on, the game could crash at a death screen (playtest, 23:06): the photo mode visibility watcher ran off the game thread every 0.25 s, finding the mod's command channel (`could not load /Script/SB.Default__SBCheatManager` over and over while the level loaded) and asking the DLL about a pawn the respawn was remaking. It now runs from her tick, so nothing of the mod's runs off the game thread at all. 0.2.4 ran that watcher for everyone, dev or not: likely the endless loading screen in the Nexus report.
- Her mouth opens farther as her face moves after the death (`convulse.faceOpen` 1.8; reported as too little): the poses drove the morphs that drop it at a fraction of their reach (a gasp's `jaw_open` 0.55 dropped her chin 1.4 cm of the morph's 2.5, a wince's 0.18 half a centimetre, a trembling jaw 3 mm), so `jaw_open`, `lowerLip_down` and `wholeMouth_down` in them now go 1.8 times as far. A groan's lips-in named a morph her face doesn't have (`mouth_in`, now `lowerLip_in`).
- A split on her death fall showed the ragdoll before the cut (reported): she ragdolled the moment the split was rolled and hung frozen in it for the ~0.4 s the cut takes to build, then fell apart. Her fall now plays on while it is cut and she ragdolls as it opens, both halves going on at the speed the fall gave them (`split open (death): ..., she ragdolls now` in the log). Untested in game.
- Her breaths and moans after the death are quieter (`convulse.voice.breathVolume` 0.25, was 0.35; `moanVolume` 0.15, was 0.3; asked for), and `settings.lua` takes `convulse.voice.volume` to scale both.
- A new death face mood, `dread` (weight 3): died frightened, one eye half shut and the other staring, brows up and uneven, the mouth twisted down and aside on one side with the lip up on the other, the jaw off to that side. Its `mirror` flag turns it to either side per death (`face (death): dread (mirrored)` in the log). Shift+F7 shows it among the others. Untested in game.
- Her neck struggles after the death (`convulse.neck`, also with `body` off): her head strains to lift or turn for 0.7-1.3 s, shaking with the effort (a 7-11 Hz tremor along the strain and half of it crossways), gives out, then jerks 2-4 times the same way, weakening, with the neck's cords and a clenched jaw on her face. On an animated death her head simulates for it, held to the pose by a weak drive and pulled back after. Log: `neck strains front for 1.1 s, shaking at 9 Hz, then 3 jerks`. Untested in game.
- Shift+F4 on the controller: D-pad down + R1 in a death holds it or lets it go (`holdPad`). Untested in game.
- `active.stiffness` (settings.lua too): how stiff her ragdoll is, times the muscle tone's strength and resting share (0 limp from the start; above 1 she keeps her shape and lands without going limp). Asked for on Nexus.
- After a respawn her skin still had the death's blood on it (reported): the blood the mod raises on her skin at a death cut (`bodyBlood`, the game's `MPC_PCDirtyOffset` collection, `BloodOffset` 0 -> 0.5) was put back only when she recovered in place.
