# Changelog

## Unreleased

- `tune.gore.probes` is off again: the stretch probe found what flashed (bones the GPU doesn't skin), and its stutter after every cut is gone.
- The leaned split shaved off the far side of her neck's lower half (playtest render): the neck plane sat halfway up the neck. It sits at the neck's base now, and her half keeps the neck whole, only the neck's width (`NECK_BASE_R`, 7 cm) under halfway to the head bone and her collar's 10 cm above; the strip under it on the far side closes into the neck under its lid. 10 cm wide at the base, it kept the far trapezius, stretched to where the dropped arm collapsed.
- The split's head half kept both arms, one cut through at the shoulder (playtest, `limbs Bip001-L-Clavicle,Bip001-R-Clavicle,...`): `limb_sides` sorted the arms by a later `LIMB_ROOTS` holding the clavicles, which sit by the neck, so a plane leaned beside it had both on one side. A split sorts them by the shoulder joint again (`SPLIT_LIMBS`, as the harness always did), its grazing check and its accessory chains too.
- The split's other half showed a flat hand: a dropped arm's vertices go to a point on the plane where it joined, riding the nearest spine bone, and for the arms that is the neck, which nothing free rides (CH_P_EVE_09/10's big sections, straight splits too), so the arm was flattened onto the plane instead. A dropped limb's point, and a dropped chain's or the deep torso's, may now ride any spine bone (`ANY_BAND`; not a corrective under a clavicle or breast, which the animation turns). The harness now fails a split that flattens any vertex of a dropped limb (it found 8532).
- Cut-off arms and legs fell like stones: for `tune.gore.pieceFloat` (3) s after the cut they fall at `pieceGravity` (0.55) of gravity, topped up every tick as a speed change on all the piece's bodies.
- Ragdoll limb cuts stretched her skin into a long sheet that spun about (playtest, CH_P_EVE_10 thigh cut, `sinks=46,...`): the hip's seam was collapsed onto `Ab-L-Elbow`, her left arm's elbow helper, which lay by the hip in the fall and won as the nearest sink once the hip's own helpers had moved off it; the seam then rode the flailing arm. A limb or head cut now keeps its sinks off every other limb (`limbs_list` minus the cut bone's own limb). Stub carriers may no longer be bones with a physics body of their own (a calf's stub rode `Ab-L-Hip`, the hip's jiggle body). Harness: `ELBOWHIP=1` thigh cuts (fail with the old rule on EVE_10 and EVE_30, `OLDNOSINK=1`), calf cuts; `test/run.sh arm` runs both on EVE_09/10/30. Untested in game.
- The same stretch on the next playtest, faces flashing on a thigh cut: with the elbow ruled out the hip's seam went to `Ab-L-Trape0`, a chest helper 44 cm away, since the hip's own correctives (`Ab-Venter`, `Ab-AX2`) were out as bones the watch saw moving (session 26c). gore.lua now sends her physics bodies for a limb or head cut (`n...,fffe,<bodies>`); a sink must ride the stump's body or the next one, a seam that would ride another borrows a still helper of the stump's body (EVE_10/30: `Ab-AX3T`; EVE_09 has none and keeps `Dm-*-Thigh-point`), a moved bone is the last resort only against two bodies away, and the seam collapses to the opening's middle 2 cm inside rather than the helper's origin. Torso cuts send no bodies and are unchanged. Harness: `HIPMOVED=1` thigh cuts near the hip and the knee with the correctives turned, failing on a sink more than one body from its stump. Untested in game.
- An F11 / F5 / odds split no longer halves her head: its plane leans about her front-to-back axis from the crotch to `tune.gore.splitBeside` cm beside her neck, so the head and neck stay whole on her half and the cut runs down through her chest (blade splits keep the blade's plane; 0 = the old straight cut). The copy's neck lace (CH_P_EVE_09, a section knowing only the neck, head and `Ab-NeckSub`) found no sink once the plane missed the neck and stood where her neck had been: a deep-torso point sink may now ride any band when its section has no flattening sink. Harness: `BESIDE=10` splits, plain and posed, on EVE_09/30. Untested in game. Playtest: at 10 cm it ran through a breast, so `splitBeside` is 2 now: through the middle of her crotch, 1.4 cm off her middle between the breasts (they sit 4.3 cm either side), 2 cm past the neck.
- Stretched faces flashing across the screen on every cut and split, somewhere new every frame (playtest screenshots, CH_P_EVE_10 thigh cut; the in-game stretch probe called those triangles hidden): the cut borrows helpers with no skin of their own on her mesh as seam sinks and stub carriers (`Dm-R-Thigh-point`, `Ab-AX3`, `Ab-AX3T`, `Dm-*-AX2-Root`), but the GPU skin rebuilds a bone's matrix each frame only for the LOD's `ActiveBoneIndices`, so their vertices (3413 on a thigh cut) were skinned with leftover data. A borrowed bone now joins `ActiveBoneIndices` (found in the LOD's render data by its contents, swapped and restored like the BoneMaps; `active=` in the cut's result), and if it can't be found no inactive bone is borrowed. The harness and test_cut model the GPU's active bones and fail a cut that leaves a vertex outside them; new `NOACTIVE=1` thigh and split jobs. `tune.gore.probes` is on for the next playtest. Untested in game.
- The game crashed on every Alt+F4 since stub carriers started borrowing bones (`MallocBinned2 Corruption Canary was 0x3941`): a BoneMap swapped for a bigger one came from `VirtualAlloc`, and the engine freed it with its own allocator on quit. The bigger array now comes from `GMalloc` (`RVA_GMALLOC`), and the restore frees what it replaces there too. The harness and test_cut use a fake GMalloc and fail if any BoneMap left is not its own.
- Hits she lives through leave a slash of blood on her skin (`tune.gore.hitMarks`, on; F10 tests it): no cut, no dent. The DLL finds the skin under the reported hit (sbslice `m`: the first skin a ray from the nearest part's bone axis leaves through, and its normal), and a deferred blood decal goes there, stretched along the enemy's swing, attached to the bone that carries that skin and reaching only half the part's radius into her, so it sits on her and never floats or shows through. Only hits that took HP off her bar, none for a killing blow. Marks fade as healing brings her HP bar back past where it was before their hit, and all go on recovery or respawn. New `mark` group in `test/run.sh` (rays four ways out of the torso, an arm and a thigh on EVE_09/10/30, plain and posed).
- The old wound system is gone: dents, ruptures and stabs (F10, the after-death hits that didn't cut, her swings through enemy corpses), their torn-flesh lids, sounds and settings (`woundScale`, `bluntWound`, `rupture*`, `blunt*`, `woundFlesh*`, `woundBleed`, `stab*`, `maxWounds`, `afterHits.stabShare`), and the DLL's Hole, Dent and Rupture modes (3, 5, 6) with their tests. Those hits leave a hit mark instead, F10 puts one on her, and `skin_cut` lost its depth argument.
- Glass cannon still never found her HP: no hit was seen, and her death check came back `err none at 0, kept 7557` (her HP wasn't 0; her HUD's HP bar read 0.044). The HP bar, `ProgressBar_HP.Percent`, is the one HUD value the game keeps live, so the search now anchors on it: once the bar holds still at a reading under 0.98, `vd<actor>,3` keeps the stats arrays whose HP / MaxHP read it (within 0.015, heals included), and finds her array when one is left; her death checks the bar before HP 0. The widget is kept between reads (FindAllOf every 5 s at most). `test_stats` covers a full bar refused, a bar reading found, a heal in between and two readings telling arrays apart. Untested in game.
- Session 26c: tall dark sheets rose from the pieces in ragdoll deaths with a split (playtest log, CH_P_EVE_10). The copy's head breaks free and spins (up to 8000 deg/s), and three things still followed it: the split lid and the flattened sheet, which rode every spine bone including the head, and the copy's neck skin, which kept part of its vanilla weight on the head (557 vertices). Each half now rides only the spine bones it keeps (`SPLIT_RIDE.of`: the copy's head and the far end of a broken spine are left out of the DLL's sheet bands, the lid, the opening's cap and the spine pieces), the copy's neck skin moves its head weight onto the neck, and bones the bone watch saw moving while she played (animation-driven correctives such as `Ab-AX3`) can't be sinks. Harness: half B flings its head too and nothing it keeps may follow; the posed lid check bends the lid as `skin_lid` does (`LIDRIGID=1` the old rigid pieces), which cleared the pre-existing posed CH_P_EVE_10 failure; `DRIVEN=1` splits fail if a turned corrective is a sink; `test/run.sh` adds CH_P_EVE_10 splits (plain, posed, driven) and a driven CH_P_EVE_09 split. Untested in game.
